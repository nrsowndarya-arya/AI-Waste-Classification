Categories & how it works

How a decision is made

  1. Your photo is resized to 224 x 224 pixels and normalised.
  2. A MobileNetV2 neural network (fine-tuned on the TrashNet dataset of 2,527 images) scores all 6 categories.
  3. The scores become probabilities. The highest one is the predicted category, and the top 3 are shown as bars.
  4. A fixed rule table maps the category to a bin colour, a recyclable / not recyclable label and a disposal tip.
  5. If the top probability is below 60%, the app warns that the model is unsure.

Accuracy on 506 unseen test images: 92.1%. Photos that look very different from the training data (for example reflective cans or cluttered scenes) can be misclassified. Bin colours are this project's convention, so always follow your local recycling rules.
